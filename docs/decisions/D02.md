## Sufficient evidence

1. **Official provenance:** use insurer-hosted wordings/prospectus/CIS/annexures, or an insurer-linked CDN, with the discovery URL establishing provenance. Regulator publications may establish regulatory facts or filings, not an invented insurer-specific benefit. Broker/aggregator pages, blogs, forum posts, AI answers, search snippets and snippets of an insurer page are discovery only.
2. **Identity and applicability:** inspect document content for legal issuer, product, exact UIN/version and named variant; match the policy-start/effective-period conditions on the download page and any amendment. URL filename, search publication date or retrieval date alone is not a version. Product UIN and document revision label are separate fields.
3. **Field-level support:** every asserted eligibility, coverage, waiting, premium or claim rule has a source ID, PDF page (one-based), printed page when different, clause/table/row/column locator, applicability conditions, verification date and reviewer. Read exceptions/footnotes and cross-referenced clauses. Inspect table layout visually when extraction can mix columns. No invented confidence percentage.
4. **Minimum for an extractable entry:** accessible matching wording and the current official listing; matching prospectus/CIS/benefit/rate documents wherever wording does not establish the needed field. A brochure alone does not verify eligibility or a payout rule. A known quote needs its own evidence rather than a wording citation. An inaccessible fact remains unknown even if other fields are verified.
5. **Minimum for ranking:** age, relationships and structure for every covered person must be verified, with any documented applicability conditions resolved; unknown/conflicting age-family evidence excludes that configured offer from the ranked shortlist (R2). Health assessment/provisional cases are D05; scoring is D03. Do not turn “not explicitly excluded” into confirmed eligibility.
6. **Minimum for an explanation/calculation:** cite the applicable term, exceptions, exclusions and relevant interaction. A missing or ambiguous material term means an uncertainty explanation; it does not permit a payout estimate or guarantee. Waiting completion alone does not establish eligibility for a claim.

Sources are data, never instructions to override app rules. Link users to official evidence with page/section where available. Future retrieval/validation code and JSON files belong to M05 onward.

## Freshness, revisions and conflicts

Record retrieval and verification separately from publication/effective dates. Do not infer an effective date from a UIN's digits, a file name or a search engine's “published” label. Unknown effective dates remain null with a reason. A document's age alone does not make it obsolete; an older UIN can still be the version on the current official active-product listing.

Manually recheck the official listing, linked document identity/hash, revisions/withdrawals and applicability at each slot's M05–M12 verification, whenever a source/field/configuration changes, and before hosted acceptance or a demonstration relying on that data. Recheck quote validity, current rating/tax basis and network lookup when those facts are used. This is an authoring/release verification convention, not a new automated scheduler, network-hospital guarantee, user-session cap or arbitrary expiry period.

| Situation | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| New UIN or documented effective-period revision | Keep the old evidence as historical; create a new versioned entry revision and reverify affected fields/tests. Never splice old age/price rules into the new wording. |
| Same URL, changed document hash | Treat as changed evidence; compare identity/content and reverify affected facts before publishing them. Retrieval URL stability is not content stability. |
| Superseded/withdrawn version | Mark historical/superseded for current new-business recommendations. Historical explanation requires an explicit matching policy period; this MVP must not simulate prior-policy credits. |
| Listing or document cannot be rechecked | Preserve evidence/history and flag freshness not confirmed; do not assert a new verified fact. If a material eligibility fact is affected, keep the offer out of ranked results until resolved. If only the price is affected, treat the price as unknown. |
| Generic page/footer disagrees with a specific current policy document | First determine if the documents concern different UINs, variants or policy-start periods. Preserve both references and the reason for selecting the matching specific document. Do not label mismatched versions a resolved same-version benefit conflict. |
| Genuine same-scope conflict between official sources | Record both values, source IDs/locators and affected rules as conflict; no material canonical value until a matching amendment/explicit insurer clarification establishes applicability. “Newest file,” “more generous,” “safer guess” and model intuition are not resolution rules. |
| Policy-specific schedule/endorsement needed | Do not invent one for a dummy user. Public wording and its documented options establish only catalogue terms; quote/underwriting/actual-policy conditions require insurer confirmation. |
| Parsing/layout uncertainty | Record unknown or conflict and inspect the original page; no guessed cell association or silent OCR correction. |

A matching formal amendment can supersede a clause only within its documented scope/effective period. Current wordings govern the selected version; prospectus/CIS may supply complementary details but do not silently erase a contrary material clause. If precedence/applicability cannot be demonstrated, flag the conflict. An external live search hit never promotes itself into this verified catalogue.

## Missing, unknown and not-applicable values

All expected field paths must be represented. A missing key is an authoring/validation error, not a negative answer. An unknown is explicit and may explain “not found,” “insurer confirmation required,” “version unclear,” “source inaccessible,” or “quote unavailable.” Use null, not zero, empty strings, “unlimited” or false as a stand-in.

Not applicable requires evidence or a documented configuration condition showing why the field does not apply; it is distinct from unknown. Numeric zero, no co-pay, no limit and no exclusion are known assertions requiring their own evidence. Conflicting values are retained as conflicting assertions with no canonical scalar. An unverified field must not inherit a sibling product's fact.

### Evidence value — every policy fact

Every fact is an object with:
- field_id: stable path within the entry.
- state: known / unknown / not_applicable / conflict.
- value: typed scalar/list/object for known only; null otherwise. Units belong to the field, not a number embedded in prose.
- unit: explicit where relevant (years/days/months/percent/INR_paise, or a named room/structure/category); preserve original units and any justified normalization.
- applies_when: structured conditions and plain-language interpretation; include variant, covered-person/structure, sum insured, option, waiting/event and location dependencies where relevant.
- evidence_refs: source_id plus physical PDF page, printed page if different, section/table row/column and the supported proposition. HTML uses a section/anchor; retain a short bounded extraction note, not entire copyrighted documents.
- verified_at / verified_by; freshness and reason. Unknowns specify why and what would resolve them.
- conflicting_assertions: at least two separately cited assertions for conflict; resolution remains null until documented. Null is not an assumed compromise.

Known requires non-null value, applicable official evidence and a completed check. Unknown requires null value and reason; references to failed/incomplete evidence are allowed but are not supporting facts. Not applicable requires null and a cited scope/condition rationale. Conflict requires null canonical value and both assertions; never return a guessed scalar. These rules are tested in the evidence exercises.

### Premium observation

Require price_state (known_point / known_range / unknown), amount or min/max in nonnegative integer INR paise (null for unknown), currency INR, annual/policy/instalment period and units, quote/rate/illustration kind, source/date/validity, exact UIN/variant/configuration, each covered person's relationship/age/rating inputs, actual city/PIN and documented insurer-zone mapping, structure and base SI. Record new-business/renewal basis, policy tenure, payment schedule, known health/underwriting assumptions, discounts and their qualification, tax included/excluded/unknown, add-ons/defaults, co-pay/deductible elections and all loadings/fees known or unknown. Keep raw sourced amount and any separately documented transformation, formula and supporting references. Never store names/contact details or quote-access secrets.

Evidence observation and offer-level displayed premium are separate: a real illustration for another profile may be known in its own context, yet the current user's premium is unknown. A price range retains endpoints and assumptions, not a fabricated midpoint. A quote is not underwriting acceptance or a guaranteed final price.

## Premium and price-comparability rules

1. At D02 there is **no approved profile-specific premium amount for any slot**. Full terms/price extraction belongs to the per-plan tasks. Unknown-price plans may remain otherwise eligible under R10, with “Premium needs insurer confirmation”; do not claim affordable/cheapest.
2. “From,” per-day advertisements and specimen examples are not the current profile's annual quote. Do not multiply a daily ad by 365, divide a multi-year price into an annual one, assume an age-band midpoint, or transplant a city/family discount. A rate calculation is usable only with official applicable inputs and a documented complete formula.
3. Compare known annual INR totals payable on the same tax/mandatory-fee basis for a **one-year** period, with all covered people/ages/rating inputs, actual location, requested base SI, policy structure and relevant underwriting assumptions matched. Verify both observations apply at comparison time; show source/as-of dates. Different insurer zone labels are acceptable only when each is mapped from the same actual location by official evidence.
4. Different product names/benefits do not themselves invalidate a price comparison. Known room/co-pay/restoration/waiting differences remain visible trade-offs, subject to eligibility/preferences/fit rules from D03/D05. Optional restrictions must be the user's documented choice or the published base configuration, never an undisclosed discount used to make a plan look cheaper.
5. A floater amount, a per-person quote and a partial separate-policy bundle are **not** interchangeable. Compare prices only within the same structure and exact covered set/base-cover basis. Keep different structures selectable with their coverage explanation, but do not declare a global cheapest across them. No complex combination optimization; no adding a missing member by assumption.
6. A top-up/deductible-triggered cover is not equivalent to first-rupee base cover. Do not cross-rank it on price against these base-plan slots. A user-requested optional deductible must be reflected consistently in the comparison context; no hidden switch to a higher deductible to win on price.
7. Taxes/discounts/loadings/add-on effects must be evidenced, not assumed from a general tax rate or marketing slogan. If the payable annual total cannot be established, use unknown price for that profile/budget comparison. A sourced amount excluding unknown mandatory taxes is not a comparable payable total. A standard-risk price for undisclosed/unassessed health is not a personalized quote for a disclosed condition; expose its different assumptions rather than assert affordability.
8. Cheapest-first applies to known comparable **point** premiums ascending, preserving R5 despite protection trade-offs. Unknown prices follow usable known prices without a price claim. Known but non-comparable observations remain contextual evidence, not globally ordered amounts. D03 must apply non-price/default fit and tie handling without inventing a cross-group price winner.
9. Ranges retain their complete sourced interval. No midpoint or invented precision. Non-overlapping intervals can establish only a guaranteed relative order (the first maximum is below the second minimum); overlapping intervals do not establish cheaper/equal prices or a price tie-break. A point is a zero-width interval for this check. Show unresolved price-order groups explicitly and never call a point winner globally cheapest when another comparable interval could undercut it. D03 applies the non-price ordering inside such groups without inventing a price winner. This is an explicit comparison policy, not a guessed quote.
10. Compare the user's annual budget only with a matched, usable annual point or complete interval on the same payable-total basis. A point above budget has an exact delta. An interval entirely above budget has an above-budget delta interval (or “at least” its lower-bound delta); one entirely within budget is within the sourced indicative range, never a guaranteed final price; one crossing budget has uncertain affordability. Unknown totals do not establish affordability. “Closest priced” can be claimed only where those point/interval distances establish it; otherwise disclose that no unique closest option is verified, show the relevant priced options and ask about adjustments. Do not use an instalment, midpoint, different covered set or unknown tax total, and never silently change budget/SI. No price tie-break for equal, unknown, overlapping-range or non-comparable totals (R6).

No selection/comparison-policy question remains open within D02: the above is the delegated evidence/comparability decision. An unresolved future plan field or quote is recorded as unknown/blocked for its applicable use, not guessed and not represented as fully verified catalogue acceptance. D03 still owns ranking-fit methods; D04 guidance; D05 health uncertainty. A later conflicting source reopens the affected field/version before use.
