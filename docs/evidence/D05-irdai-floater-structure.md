## Interpretation and decision boundary

- **Inference:** the checked IRDAI public guidance defines the shared-limit concept and leaves the actual allowed members/eligibility to the insurer's product terms; it does not publish one universal roster for every floater. The app must therefore evaluate each exact current product wording, variant and UIN. Do not describe the user-facing list as “IRDAI-approved relatives.”
- The proposer must be older than 18 under the owner's app rule. The reviewed IRDAI guidance does not establish this as a universal family-floater proposer-age rule; never attribute it to IRDAI.
- The owner permits a proposer/payer who is not insured and who may be outside the family. IRDAI's checked general guidance does not guarantee that every insurer accepts every third-party proposer/payer arrangement. Verify the exact proposal requirements; otherwise label acceptance as needing insurer confirmation. The app's role model is not an underwriting acceptance.
- Owner-defined structure permits a spouse, own parents and children in the shared plan, but requires a father-in-law to use a separate plan; an adult father-in-law may be the proposer. This is the owner's product behavior, not an IRDAI relationship rule, and each separate product/proposer arrangement still requires current insurer-source verification.
- For each considered product, catalogue evidence must source-check relationship, age, dependency, member-count and structure terms. Stale or UIN-mismatched documents cannot qualify a person.
- If a product cannot cover the requested set in one floater, only separately verified policy/structure arrangements may be shown; each must name its covered people and own SI. This follows the owner decision and SPEC R12. No combination is verified by the regulatory spot check alone.
- **Age-out renewal boundary:** do not say the child's policy must simply terminate or that they lose continuity. If the exact wording no longer allows the child as a floater dependent at the threshold, show a separate policy/migration path, use current IRDAI renewal/migration guidance for continuity explanation, and flag insurer-specific terms for confirmation. The regulatory check does not identify the child's exit age for any D02 catalogue product.
